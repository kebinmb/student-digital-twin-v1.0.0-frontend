Troubleshooting Guidelines

  • Indoors: Thick building walls may attenuate satellite GPS. SDT automatically falls back to cellular/Wi-Fi positioning.
  • HTTP vs HTTPS: Android and iOS browsers automatically block location sensor access over plain HTTP IP addresses.
  • Campus Bounds: If GPS is unavailable, attendance check-in still succeeds with campus centroid bounds.
